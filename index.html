Landmark 81 — Three Ways In

Home page: a dusk reconstruction of Landmark 81 in Vinhomes Central Park, Ho Chi Minh City — 461.2 metres, 81 floors, the tallest tower in Vietnam. Three entrances are marked and described. Scroll to travel from the overview to entrance 01, then 02 and 03 — the camera eases along the route and settles at each entrance. Scroll up to reverse, drag to orbit, pinch to zoom.

The building: step through any entrance and the landmark gives way to a Backrooms office level — fluorescent yellow, damp carpet, one very long corridor. Three thresholds are marked inside. Scroll (or press 1–3) and the camera walks the corridors to the next threshold; WASD still takes over at any time, drag to look, Shift to run.

Routes: every view has an address — #/exterior, #/entrance/1–3, and #/floor/1–3 — so the floor and each entrance can be linked or bookmarked directly.

Models: Landmark 81 (3D scan, supplied) and the Backrooms VR environment (supplied). Lighting, atmosphere, window lights, interface and interaction built in Three.js / WebGL. This is a concept experience.
